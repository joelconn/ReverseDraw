# Privacy Policy for ReverseDraw

**Effective Date:** September 29, 2026

## Overview

ReverseDraw ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and otherwise handle your information in connection with our macOS and iPad application (the "App").

## Information We Collect

### Local Data Only

ReverseDraw is designed with privacy-first principles. **We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers.**

All data used by ReverseDraw is stored locally on your device:
- Event configuration and settings
- Ticket numbers and drawing history
- Guest list information (names, sponsor levels)
- Event archives and results
- Application preferences

### Data We Do NOT Collect

- ❌ No account or sign-in data
- ❌ No analytics or usage tracking
- ❌ No device identifiers or advertising IDs
- ❌ No location data
- ❌ No IP addresses or network data
- ❌ No personal information transmission to external servers

## Data Storage

### On Your Device
- All event data is stored locally in your macOS or iPad file system
- Location: `~/Library/Application Support/RotaryDraw/`
- Data persists between app sessions
- You have full control over this data

### Backup
- Event archives are saved as JSON files in your local file system
- You can export and backup these files manually
- No automatic cloud backup is performed

## Data Access & Sharing

### Who Has Access
- Only you have access to your event data
- Data is not shared with third parties
- Data is not transmitted to our servers or any external service

### Third-Party Services
ReverseDraw does not integrate with:
- Cloud services (iCloud, Google Drive, OneDrive, etc.)
- Analytics platforms (Google Analytics, Mixpanel, etc.)
- Advertising networks
- Social media platforms
- Data brokers

## Security

### Data Protection
- Your data is protected by your device's operating system security
- File system permissions restrict access to the app's data directory
- We recommend keeping your macOS and iPad systems updated with security patches

### What We Recommend
- Keep your device's operating system up to date
- Use device-level security (password, biometric authentication)
- Back up your data regularly to external storage if important

## Your Rights & Controls

### Access Your Data
- All your data is stored in plain text JSON format
- You can access it directly at: `~/Library/Application Support/RotaryDraw/`
- You can export, backup, or delete data anytime

### Delete Your Data
- Uninstalling the app does not automatically delete stored data
- To remove all data: Delete the `RotaryDraw` folder in Application Support
- Or use the in-app "Exit & Save" function and delete the backup files

### Data Portability
- Event archives are exported as standard JSON files
- You can view, copy, or move these files freely
- No vendor lock-in

## Children's Privacy

ReverseDraw does not target children under 13. The app is suitable for all ages as it contains no inappropriate content, but:
- We do not knowingly collect data from children
- We do not market to children
- Parents/guardians have full visibility and control over all data used

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the effective date.
